Steinar H. Gunderson, (Sesse)
a Norwegian computer scientist and software engineer, Debian developer and ex-demoscener 1. While currently affiliated with the Oracle Corporation in the MySQL optimizer team, he previously worked at Google Switzerland on projects such as IPv6, Snappy, and Search by Image 2. He holds a master in CS from Norwegian University of Science and Technology in Trondheim on the topic of music information retrieval 3. Steinar H. Gunderson has a wide range of interests spanning from DSP and computer chess to high-level languages and networking architecture. He has contributed to various FOSS projects 4.

Computer Chess

In 2007, Sesse published the unofficial CTG specification (ChessBase Opening Tree of Games) 5. He is author of remoteglot 6, a program used to use a UCI compatible chess engine to analyze chess games, including live online relay. It is used for his live chess analysis website, known as Sesse 7. He has further written the UCI compliant engine Stupid 8, hosts Nalimov Tablebases and Syzygy Bases 9, and is contributor to the Stockfish project 10.
