Feng-hsiung Hsu, (Feng-Hsiung Hsu)
a Taiwanese electrical engineer, computer scientist and creator of the VLSI-architecture 1 of the chess entities ChipTest, Deep Thought 2 and Deep Blue 3, which defeated Garry Kasparov in 1997 4. Feng-hsiung Hsu came to the United States after graduating from National Taiwan University with a B.S. in electrical engineering 5. In 1985, he started with the chip design as Ph.D. Student at the Carnegie Mellon University, defending his Ph.D. thesis on large scale parallelization of alpha-beta search in 1989 under supervision of Hsiang-Tsung Kung 6 and was then hired by IBM for the Deep Blue project 7.

Hsu now manages the platforms and devices center of Microsoft Research Asia, in Beijing 8 .
