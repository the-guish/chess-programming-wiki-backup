Karlo Balla, (Karlo Balla Jr., Karlo Bala Jr.)
a Serbian computer scientist and assistant professor at Department of Media Studies, Faculty of Philosophy, University of Novi Sad, where he already obtained his Diploma and Master's degrees in CS in 2004 and 2010 respectively. His research interests include combinatorial optimization, artificial intelligence, logistics, data structures and algorithms, and E-learning. As a chess player 1, he is further interested in chess programming, also working on an own, private engine 2.
