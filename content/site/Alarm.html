Alarm, (Deamon)
a WinBoard compliant chess engine by Benny Antonsson and Erik Robertsson, written in C++. Alarm, the Diabolic Chess Engine 1, was first released in July 2003 2, and was first mentioned in 2001 formerly called Deamon 3. Alarm played four CCT Tournaments from 2003 to 2006, the CCT5, CCT6, CCT7 and the CCT8.
