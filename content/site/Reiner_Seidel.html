Reiner Seidel, (Rainer Seidel 1 2)
a German psychologist, associate professor at Free University of Berlin 3, chess player and chess theorist who coined a scientific Chess theory as introduced in his 1987 book Grundlagen einer wissenschaftlichen Schachtheorie 4, also topic of two Advances in Computer Chess Conferences and various ICCA Journal articles.
