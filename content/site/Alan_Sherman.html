Alan T. Sherman,
an American computer scientist, cryptographer, and full professor of computer science at University of Maryland, Baltimore County. He earned his M.Sc. and Ph.D. under Ronald L. Rivest at MIT in 1981 and 1987 respectively. His research interests include high-integrity voting systems, information assurance, cryptology, and discrete algorithms. Alan Sherman has been the faculty advisor of the UMBC Chess Club since 1991 1 2 3.
