Mike Leahy,
an American computer scientist, programmer and author of the opening database Bookup along with its Chess Openings Wizard GUI. He also created the Bobby Fischer Teaches Chess CD ROM 1 2 based on the chess-book of the same name by Bobby Fischer, incorporating Richard Lang's Chess Genius 3 4.
