ACCA 2012

Home * Tournaments * ACCA Americas' Computer Chess Championship * ACCA 2012

< Prev

The 2012 Seventh Annual ACCA Americas' Computer Chess Championships was played November 17 & 18, 2012 on ICC, Round Robin Standard, 45min 10sec increment. Six programs participated. Interestingly, the final ranking was in alphabetic order, the winner was Arasan by Jon Dart.
