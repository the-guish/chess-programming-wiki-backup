William T. B. Uther,
an Australian computer scientist at NICTA and School of Computer Science and Engineering, University of New South Wales (UNSW) with research interests in Reinforcement Learning, Robot Soccer and Autonomous Driving. He defended his Ph.D in Computer Science at Carnegie Mellon University in 2002 1.
