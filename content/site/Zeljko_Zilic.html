Zeljko Zilic,
a Croatian Canadian electrical engineer and computer scientist, and associate professor at McGill University. He holds a B. Eng from University of Zagreb and received his Ph.D. and M.Sc. from the University of Toronto. His research interests covers logic synthesis, testing, verification and debug FPGA, embedded, wireless and multiprocessor systems as well as algebraic, combinatorial and quantum algorithms 1.
