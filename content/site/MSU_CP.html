MSU CP

Home * Engines * MSU CP

MSU CP,
the Mississippi State University chess program, written by Mike Rackley and George Moore. MSU CP, apparently a Shannon Type B program, played the ACM 1972, where it ran on a 36-bit UNIVAC 1106 1.
