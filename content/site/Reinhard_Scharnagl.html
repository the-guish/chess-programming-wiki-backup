Reinhard Scharnagl, (died in August 2015)
was a German computer chess programmer, and author of the chess program SMIRF. Reinhard was pioneer in Fischer Random Chess (FRC / Chess960) 1 as well for chess variants with other board sizes like 10x8. On August 01, 2015 he posted on his Google+ site that he survived three weeks of chemotherapy and radiation therapy fighting brain tumor 2. He died soon later that month in August 2015 3 4.
