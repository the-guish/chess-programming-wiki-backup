Andreas Mader,
an Austrian computer chess expert, tester and journalist. He was member of the Nimzo team 1 around Chrilly Donninger, and the associated First Vienna Computer Chess Club (Nimzo Wekstatt), primary editor of the computer chess periodicals Modul and PC Schach, and one of the founders of CCC 2.
