CCT Tournaments

Home * Tournaments * CCT Tournaments

The Programmer's Computer Chess Tournament CCT are Online Computer Chess Tournaments played via the Internet Chess Club (ICC) 1 , and since 2010 via the Free Internet Chess Server (FICS) 2 .
