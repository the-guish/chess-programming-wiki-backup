Bareev versus HIARCS Match,
between Evgeny Bareev (2729 and world's number eight player, peak rating of 2739, and world's number 4 in October 2003 1) and HIARCS 8X took place in Centre Céramique, Maastricht, The Netherlands, from January 28 to 31, 2003. The four games match was organized by the Chess Events Maastricht Foundation 2. HIARCS 8X operated by its author Mark Uniacke ran on an AMD Athlon 2.0 GHz with 1GiB RAM 3. After four draws, the match ended in a 2-2 tie.
