WCRCC 2010

Home * Tournaments * ACCA World Computer Rapid Chess Championship * WCRCC 2010

The Fourth Annual ACCA World Computer Rapid Chess Championship was held July 17-18, 2010. The format was Rapid Chess (G/25+4), played on-line over the internet (ICC).
