Ilya Sutskever,
a Canadian computer scientist, and co-founder and research director of OpenAI, a non-profit AI research company. Before, he was research scientist at Google Brain, and co-founded DNNresearch 1. He was postdoc at Stanford University with Andrew Ng's group, and received his M.Sc. in 2007 2 and his Ph.D. in 2013 3 from University of Toronto, working with Geoffrey Hinton. His research interests include machine learning and neural networks, in particular deep learning with convolutional neural networks 4 applied to machine translation, voice and image recognition, and also to the game of Go 5 .
