Edmund Moshammer,
an Austrian economist currently at the European Stability Mechanism, holding a M.Sc. degree in specialized economic analysis from Barcelona Graduate School of Economics, Barcelona, Catalonia, Spain from 2014.

As avocational chess programmer Edmund Moshammer has been involved in the development of the CPW-Engine and Glass Chess Engine, working with Pawel Koziol 1. A side project was the creation of the Glass opening book format and the corresponding tool, the Glass Opening Book Manager 2. In March 2014, Edmund Moshammer introduced a port of UCI to JavaScript dubbed JSUCI 3 4.
