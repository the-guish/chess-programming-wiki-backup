Marcel Vanthoor

Home * People * Marcel Vanthoor

Marcel Vanthoor,
a Dutch software engineer and computer chess programmer. He has already written a very basic chess program in Turbo Pascal 1, and started a chess programming comeback in 2020 with his didactic chess engine Rustic written in Rust 2. Progress on Rustic is topic of his CCC thread, explaining all his development trials 3.
