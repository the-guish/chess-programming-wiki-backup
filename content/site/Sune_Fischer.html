Sune Fischer,
a Danish physicist, computer scientist and full-stack .NET developer with experience and interests in solution architecture, cloud computing, code quality, agile processes, automated testing and CI\CD\DevOps 1. He holds a Bachelor in physics in 2001 from University of Copenhagen on simulated annealing and travelling salesman problem, covering Metropolis–Hastings algorithm, thermodynamics and statistical mechanics, and further worked on neural networks. As a chess player and computer chess programmer, Sune Fischer is author of the Chess Engine Communication Protocol and UCI compatible chess engine Frenzee, previously known as ChessCraft 2. Along with Pradu Kannan, he contributed to the Chess Programming Wiki in 2007 with the examination on Pawn Advantage, Win Percentage, and Elo.
