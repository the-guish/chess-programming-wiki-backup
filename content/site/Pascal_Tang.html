Pascal Tang,
a French computer scientist, software engineer, games developer, and CEO and co-founder of Datagacy 1, a company providing solutions for deep technical issues 2. Pascal holds a diplôme d'études supérieures spécialisées in computer science from University of Western Brittany in 1994. In 1996/97 he was affiliated with Titus France as project manager of Virtual Chess II, which he represented and operated at the WMCCC 1996 in Jakarta, and the WMCCC 1997 in Paris 3. Pascal Tang is primary author of the the world class Chinese Chess program XieXie and the chess engine Xie Long 4, and worked with primary author Eugenio Castillo Jiménez on the chess program Chinito 5, which he operated at the WCCC 2002 in Maastricht.
