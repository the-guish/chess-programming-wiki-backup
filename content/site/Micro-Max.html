Micro-Max, (μ-Max)
a minimalist open source chess program in up to 2000 characters and 133 lines of portable C source-code by Harm Geert Muller. Recently Micro-Max was ported for an Atmel-ATmega88 Microcontroller 1 by Andre Adrian using WinAVR GCC, deployed for a self-made chess computer SHAH 2 and the ATM18 mini chess computer from the electronics magazine Elektor.
