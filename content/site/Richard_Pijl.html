Richard Pijl,
a Dutch computer scientist, M.Sc. from Delft University of Technology, and computer chess programmer. He is author of the chess engines The Baron and Crashtest Dummy. Since June 2008 Richard organized the The Chess Programmers Tournament in Belgium, where he actually lives near the Dutch border. From December 2012 until 2016 he was the secretary of the CSVN board.
