IGWT 2014

Home * Tournaments * Italian Computer Chess Championship * IGWT 2014

< Prev Next >

The IGWT 2014, the International Gsei Web Tournament II edition, took place in May/June 2014 1. After various qualification steps, 8 Italian and 8 guest engines played the knockout, won by title holder Chiron in the final versus Gaviota.
