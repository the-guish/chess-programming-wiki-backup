Vitruvius, (VitruviusChess)
a commercial, UCI compatible chess engine built on the skeleton structure of the free Ippolit programs, that is using IvanHoe and its derivative Deep Saros as code base 1, with a speculative evaluation and playing style. The Vitruvius team consists of programmer Roberto Munter, book author Andrea Manzo and principal tester Domenico Lattanzi 2. Vitruvius won the 3rd Italian Open Chess Software Cup 2012 3.
