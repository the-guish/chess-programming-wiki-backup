Yves De Billoëz,
a Belgian ICT architect, manager and consultant with expertise in computer security, cryptography and banking operations 1. He is CTO and Co-Founder of eSureCoin.com, Democratic Republic of the Congo, providing banks and financial institutions the ability to offer Bitcoin purchase and sale services 2. As avocational computer chess programmer, Yves De Billoëz is author of the open source engine Belofte.
