Alexander Lyashuk,
a Belarusian computer scientist and software engineer at Google Switzerland. He graduated in 2005 from Belarusian State University in Minsk. Alexander Lyashuk is primary developer of Leela Chess Zero, in particular the rewritten Lc0 engine 1 2, which replaced the initial LCZero, running up to ten times faster on Nvidia GPUs 3.
