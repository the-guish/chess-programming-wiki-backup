Truong-Huy Dinh Nguyen,
a Vietnamese computer scientist and software engineer at Google, Ph.D. from Singapore Management University in 2013. His research lies at the cross-junction of various research fields, including data analytics, machine learning, artificial intelligence, and behavior analytics, with favorite applications being games and robotics 1. Along with Wee Sun Lee et al., he researched and published on Monte-Carlo Tree Search and UCT variant called UCT-Aux, an enhancement technique with a heuristic policy to speed up the selection of optimal actions, which augments the look-ahead tree with auxiliary arms that are evaluated by the heuristic policy 2. __FORCETOC__
