Tamerlane,
a WinBoard compliant chess engine by Luigi Marco Ripamonti, written in C++. Its development started in 2000 and Tamerlane was first released in April 2001 1. It was the author's first attempt to write "something" capable of playing chess 2, soon followed by Madeleine in 2002 3 .
