Holger Hopp,
a German computer scientist who defended his diploma thesis in 1995 under Peter Sanders on the topic of parallel search 1, and his Ph.D. thesis in 2001 on cache behaviour of optimizing compiler under Walter F. Tichy 2, both degrees from University of Karlsruhe.

Selected Publications

3

  • Holger Hopp (1995). Parallele Spielbaumsuche auf SIMD Rechnern. Diplomarbeit, University of Karlsruhe, advisor Peter Sanders (German)
  • Holger Hopp, Peter Sanders (1995). Parallel Game Tree Search on SIMD Machines. IRREGULAR 1995, pdf
  • Holger Hopp (2001). Vorhersage des Cache-Verhaltens für optimierende Übersetzer. Ph.D. thesis, University of Karlsruhe, advisor Walter F. Tichy (German)
