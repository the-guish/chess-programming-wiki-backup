Jana Krivec,
a Slovenian chess woman grandmaster, computer scientist and researcher at Department of Intelligent systems 1, Jožef Stefan Institute, Ljubljana. Previously, she was affiliated with the Artificial Intelligence Laboratory 2 at University of Ljubljana. Her research interests covers machine learning and automated explanation / tutoring systems.
