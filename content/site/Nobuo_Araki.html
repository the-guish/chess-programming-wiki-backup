Nobuo Araki,
a Japanese computer scientist, and M.Sc. in 2008 from School of Information Science and Technology, University of Tokyo, and Ph.D. student at University of Electro-Communications where his research on computer Go is supported by the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science. He is author of MC_ark, a Go playing program performing MCTS, inspired by Rémi Coulom's CGW 2007 paper Computing Elo ratings of move patterns in the game of Go 1 2.
