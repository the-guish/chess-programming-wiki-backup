Philip Andrew (Phil) Lisiecki,
an American computer scientist and chief architect at Akamai Technologies 1. While affiliated with Massachusetts Institute of Technology during the late 90s, he was member of the team in developing the CilkChess massive parallel computer chess program 2 .
