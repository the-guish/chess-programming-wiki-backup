Viljem Žumer,
a Slovenian electrical engineer, computer scientist, professor and former head of the Computer Architecture and Languages Laboratory 1, Institute of Computer Science at University of Maribor. His research interests include parallel computing, multi-objective optimization, and evolutionary computation, in particular differential evolution as applied in automated tuning of chess engines.
