Stephen David (Steve) Crocker, (born October 15, 1944)
an American computer scientist, internet pioneer, and inventor of the Request for Comments (RFC) series 1. He holds a B.Sc. and Ph.D from University of California, Los Angeles in 1968 and 1977 respectively. He was part of the team that developed the protocols for the ARPANET which were the foundation for today's internet. Earlier in the 60s, he was affiliated with MIT 2, and worked with Richard Greenblatt and Donald Eastlake on The Greenblatt Chess Program or Mac Hack VI 3.
