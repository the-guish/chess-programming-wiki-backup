Tyro,
a chess program by Albert Zobrist and Frederic Roy Carlson, affiliated with the University of Southern California and the Jet Propulsion Laboratory. Tyro was successor of USC CP 1 and participated at three ACM North American Computer Chess Championships, ACM 1974, ACM 1975, and at ACM 1977 running on a PDP-10 KL10 2.
