Computer Chess

While affiliated with the Department of Systems Engineering at Australian National University, Jonathan Baxter co-authored the TD-learning chess program KnightCap 6 and further wrote his own engine TDChess based on KnightCap 7, which won the NC3 1999 Australasian National Computer Chess Championship 8. After the AlphaZero incident in December 2017, Jonathan Baxter contributed a Monte-Carlo Tree Search wrapper for Stockfish 9.
