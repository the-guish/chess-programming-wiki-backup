CPT 2010

The third Chess Programmers Tournament, was organized in March 2010 by Richard Pijl. It took place in De Bouwhoeve, Hoogstraten-Wortel, Belgium. Eleven participants, six round swiss, time-control was 90 minutes per side and game.
