All the Right Moves

As Ph.D. student at Carnegie Mellon University, Carl Ebeling was member of the HiTech team around Hans Berliner. Carl Ebeling's Ph.D. thesis was titled All the Right Moves 1 and describes a VLSI architecture for computing complex chess evaluation functions quickly, as used in HiTech.

Quotes

Hans Berliner on Ebeling's role in the HiTech team (1988) 3 :

Carl Ebeling (now at University of Washington) built the special purpose hardware (Ebeling 1985), and a good deal of software relating to how to interface to the hardware and see what it is doing for debugging purposes.
