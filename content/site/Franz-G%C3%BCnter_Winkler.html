Franz-Günter Winkler,
an Austrian physicist, computer scientist and AI researcher with interests in cognitive science 1, interpretation of Relativity theory 2, spacetime holism 3, and philosophy of time 4 and mind 5. He defended his Ph.D. thesis on spacetime holism at Vienna University of Technology in 2004 6.
