Dennis W. Cooper,
an American electrical engineer, computer scientist and early computer chess programmer. Along with his fellow Ed Kozdrowicki, Dennis Cooper already started chess programming in the 60s at University of Washington. Their program Coko, later affiliated with the Department of Electrical Engineering, University of California, Davis 1, played the very first ACM 1970 as well the ACM 1971, ACM 1972 and ACM 1973 respectively, while Cooper was already associated with the Bell Telephone Laboratories, Whippany, N.J.. Cooper's further research interests covers computerized adaptive testing, and geotechnical engineering 23.
