Berserk,
an UCI compliant open source chess engine by Jay Honnold, written in C and licensed under the GPL v3.0, first released in February 2021 1. Berserk 4.2.0-dev had its tournament debut at TCEC Season 21 in May 2021 and gained a respectable 50% score in its strong Qualification League. Version 5 marked the end of Hand Crafted Evaluation in Berserk. Berserk 6 and following feature NNUE evaluation with an own network architecture 2.
