Árpád Rusz,
a Romanian chess player 1 and chess endgame study composer 2 . He contributed along with Guy Haworth on Position Criticality in Chess Endgames at the Advances in Computer Games 13 conference in Tilburg 3 , demonstrating the implementation of their algorithm to help analyze uniqueness in endgame positions objectively with a chess variant called Starchess 4 , invented by László Polgár in 2002, which has a star-shaped board of 37 hexagons 5.
