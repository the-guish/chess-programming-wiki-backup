1K ZX Chess,
a chess program for the Sinclair ZX81, written in 1982 by David Horne in Z80 Assembly with a code size of 672 bytes, including the user interface, as published with listing in Your Computer, February 1983 1 . 1K ZX Chess was commercially market by the author himself 2, and soon by Artic Computing 3 4 , a software development company set up by Richard Turner and Chris A. Thornton 5 , a chess programmer himself, and author of Spectrum Chess 6 , which competed at the European MCC 1982 and 1983, and Turbo Chess for the ZX Spectrum 7 .

1K ZX Chess was further released by Sinclair Research in 1982 8 . The program could only play white starting either with 1.e4 or 1.d4. Castling, promotions and en passant were not implemented, while a version for the unexpanded 2K Timex Sinclair 1000 include these moves 9 .
