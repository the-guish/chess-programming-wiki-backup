Wojciech Jaśkowski,
a Polish computer scientist and researcher with Jürgen Schmidhuber at NNAISENSE 1, until 2017 assistant professor at the Institute of Computing Science, Poznań University of Technology, where he already defended his Ph.D. thesis in 2011, devoted to a subclass of optimization problems applied to learning game strategies and combinatorial optimizations 2 3. His research interests include genetic programming, coevolutionary algorithms, evolutionary computation, reinforcement learning, and pattern recognition, using the game of Othello as testbed.
