Gian-Carlo Pascutto, (GCP)
a Belgian 1 computer scientist, and computer chess and Go programmer with further interests in sound recording and reproduction and digital signal processing 2. Gian-Carlo is author of the free open source chess and chess variants engine Sjeng 3, the chess engine Deep Sjeng, which emerged from the closed source Sjeng 12.7 branch 4 since 2003 commercial 5, the experimental chess engine Stoofvlees, and the Go playing program Leela 6.

Leela Zero

More recently, after DeepMind published the AlphaGo Zero paper 7, Gian-Carlo Pascutto designed and initiated Leela Zero 8, a Go playing entity using MCTS (but without Monte Carlo playouts) and a deep residual convolutional neural network stack, trained by a public, distributed effort, as Leela Chess Zero also adapted to chess 9 10.
