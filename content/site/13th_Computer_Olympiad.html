13th Computer Olympiad

Home * Tournaments * Computer Olympiad * 13th Computer Olympiad

< Prev Next >

The venue of the Championships and Conference 1

  1. Image clipped from 5th Day Image 6, 2008 Gallery by Hideki Kato↩︎

The Thirteenth International Computer Games Championship, due to the 2008 Summer Olympics the Computer Olympiad was officially renamed for this occassion, took place from September 28 to October 5, 2008, at Beijing Golden Century Golf Club, Qinglonghu Township, Fangshan District, Beijing, China, in conjunction with the 16th World Computer Chess Championship and the 6th International Conference on Computers and Games. The organization was in the hands of Xinhe Xu supported by local staff and the approved ICGA team of Jaap van den Herik, Mark Winands, and Joke Hellemons 1 .
