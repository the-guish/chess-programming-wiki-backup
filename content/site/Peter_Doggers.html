Peter Doggers,
a Dutch chess player and chess journalist, Master of Arts in Dutch language and literature 1. Between 2007 and 2013 Peter Doggers was running ChessVibes, in 2013 acquired by Chess.com 2. Peter Doggers continued his work as chess journalist at Chess.com. His postings include multiple computer chess topics.
