Francesco Bidoia,
an Italian computer scientist, and senior robotics engineer at Ascent Robotics, Inc., Tokyo, Japan 1. He holds a B.Sc. degree on mechatronics, robotics, and automation engineering from University of Bologna in 2014, and a M.Sc. degree on artificial intelligence from University of Groningen in 2017 2, where his research focused on autonomous robots and navigation, in conjunction with reinforcement learning and deep learning. Along with Matthia Sabatelli, Valeriu Codreanu and Marco Wiering, Francesco Bidoia co-authored on Learning to Evaluate Chess Positions with Deep Neural Networks and Limited Lookahead 3. __FORCETOC__
