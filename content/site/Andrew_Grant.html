Andrew Grant,
an American computer science and mathematics major at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County 1. He is author of the UCI compliant open source chess engine Ethereal, first officially released in June 2016 2, and the distributed SPRT testing framework for chess engines, OpenBench 3 4. On June 04, 2021, Andrew Grant announced the commercial release of Ethereal 13.00 (NNUE), while the standard version remained available on Github 5. In 2023 he became team lead of Torch, the Chess.com chess engine.6
