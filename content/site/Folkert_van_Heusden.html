Folkert van Heusden,
a Dutch software developer 1. As open source and game programmer, Folkert is author of the experimental chess programs Pos 2 and DeepBrutePos 3, both written in Java. A incarnation of DeepBrutePos, running on a system with an UDOO computer and seven Raspberry Pi calculating nodes is dubbed PuppetMaster 4 . Former chess programs were Embla (in 2016 incorporated inside a self made chess computer board based on a Raspberry Pi 3 5), CPP1 6 , Bullucks 7 and QueenBee. His latest chess engine, first released in June 2019, is the UCI compliant Micah 8. He started writing chess programs on May 21, 1996 (aprox 14:30). He further wrote the Go playing program Stop 9. Other new chess programs: Feeks, CaffeinatedPawn, Dolly and Dog. He started with chess programming on March 12, 1995 (first save around 18:28:14 UTC+1).
