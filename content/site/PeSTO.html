PeSTO,
an experimental chess engine by Ronald Friederich with only minimal chess knowledge implemented, and therefore depending on alpha-beta search as much as possible 1. PeSTO performs a tapered eval to interpolate by current fine grained game stage between piece-square tables values for opening and endgame. The tables were optimized by Texel's tuning method. PeSTO played the TCEC Season 17 - and two times as lucky loser, even reached the CPU League 1.
