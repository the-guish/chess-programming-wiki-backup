Shane Samole,
an American entrepreneur and businessmen in the consumer electronics industry, and director of the B&D Group in Miami, Florida 1 2. As son of Sidney Samole, he was in the dedicated chess computer business since 1987 as Fidelity Electronics sales manager and in 1992 as founder and president of Excalibur Electronics. Until 2009, Shane Samole was owner and president of the World Chess Hall of Fame and Sidney Samole Museum 3 4. __FORCETOC__
