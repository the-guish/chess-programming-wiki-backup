Victor Zakharov,
a Russian computer scientist holding a Ph.D. in CS from Lomonosov Moscow State University, entrepreneur, founder of ChessOK aka Convekta, head of its development, primary developer of Chess Assistant 1 and Aquarium 2, and scientific secretary at the Institute of Informatics Problems of the Russian Academy of Sciences, Moscow 3. Also affiliated with the Lomonosov Moscow State University, Victor Zakharov is along with Vladimir Makhnychev involved in generating the 7-men Lomonosov Tablebases 4, using the Lomonosov Supercomputer. As of December 31, 2013, the tablebases are commercially available online through ChessOK products 5.
