Odd Gunnar Malin,
a Norwegian software developer, chess player and computer chess programmer, residing in Vadsø, Finnmark country 1. Odd Gunnar is author of the WinBoard compatible open source chess engine PolarChess including the PolarEngine package with search and evaluation decoupled from the board representation, various protocol utilities and converter such as InBetween and Wb2UCI, as well as Chess Database related tools and programs like NorBase, a game-database for games played in Norwegian chess tournaments 2.
