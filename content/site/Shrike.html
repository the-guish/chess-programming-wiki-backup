Shrike,
a WinBoard compliant private chess engine by Dan Newman, written in C++ 1. Shrike applies a non-rotated bitboard approach to frequently scan the board for sliding piece attack and move generation 2. Shrike played the first three CCT Tournaments, CCT1, CCT2, and CCT3. At CCT1, running on a Pentium III, 500 MHz, Shrike searched about 500,000 nodes per second 3.
