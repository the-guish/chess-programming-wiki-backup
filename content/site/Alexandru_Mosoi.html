Alexandru Moșoi,
a Romanian computer scientist, software engineer, and proponent of free and open source software, actually residing in Zurich working for Google Switzerland. He holds a M.Sc. degree from Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam in 2011 on the topic of distibuted SAT solving 1, and was affiliated with Politehnica University of Bucharest. Beside multiple other open source programs and projects, he is author of Chile, a compression program based on Burrows-Wheeler transform 2, the Conflict-Driven Clause Learning Sat Solver Gasca 3, the UCI compliant open source chess engine and chess library Zurichess 4 written in Go 5, and txt, which applies Texel's tuning method by Peter Österlund to optimize any UCI complaint chess engine, also written in Go 6.
