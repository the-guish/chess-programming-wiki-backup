Takuya Hiraoka, (平岡 拓也)
a Japanese computer scientist affiliated with the NEC Data Science Research Laboratories. He holds a B.Sc. in informatics from Kindai University in 2011, and a M.Sc. and Ph.D. in information science from Nara Institute of Science and Technology in 2013 and 2016 respectively 1. His research interests include dialogue system, reinforcement learning and game theory, and computer Shogi. He is author of the Shogi playing program Apery 2, initially written in C++, recently ported to Rust 3. Along with Takenobu Takizawa, Takeshi Ito and Kunihito Hoki, he published on Contemporary Computer Shogi 4.
