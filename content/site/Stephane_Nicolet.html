Stéphane Nicolet,
a French world-class Othello player, two times vice-world champion in 1996 and 2007 1, computer scientist, teacher and researcher affiliated with University of Paris II Panthéon-Assas 2, and computer Othello and chess programmer. He is primary author of the Othello program Cassio for Macintosh 3. In 2014, Stéphane Nicolet started to work on Stockfish, and became a primary member of the Stockfish community, contributing in all areas with focus on evaluation, tuning and testing.
