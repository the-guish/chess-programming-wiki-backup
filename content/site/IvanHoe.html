IvanHoe,
an UCI compliant open source chess engine of the Ippolit and RobboLito series of programs, first released in January 2010 and base of a lot of further derivatives. IvanHoe features a SMP search, Multi-PV, and Chess960 1, and uses a decrementing versioning scheme. Authors are the pseudonymous Ippolit comrades, Yakov Petrovich Golyadkin 2, Igor Igorovich Igoronov, Roberto Pescatore, Yusuf Ralf Weisskopf, and Ivan Skavinsky Skavar 3. Izak Pretorius alias PeterPan kept computer chess aficionados and testers busy with IvanHoe "compiles" 4, and the release of the IvanHoe 46h derivative PanChess. Franklin Titus alias kingliveson 5 provides source and executables as well 6.
