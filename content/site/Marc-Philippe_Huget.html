Marc-Philippe Huget,
a French computer scientist and associate professor at the University of Savoy. He received his Ph.D. from University of Paris 9 Dauphine in 2001 1. His research interests include distributed artificial intelligence, machine learning, reinforcement learning, and in the field of agent-oriented software engineering, modelling notations, methodologies and the use of model-driven engineering for developing multiagent systems for games and entertainment. As a former computer chess programmer, Marc-Philippe Huget is author of the WinBoard compliant chess engine La Dame Blanche which came along with a book builder 2 3.
