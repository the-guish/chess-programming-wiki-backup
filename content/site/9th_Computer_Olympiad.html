Psychology Building, Bar-Ilan University 1

  1. Psychology building in Bar-Ilan University, Image by Avishai Teicher via the PikiWiki - Israel free image collection project, December 20, 2011, CC BY 2.5, Wikimedia Commons, Bar-Ilan University from Wikipedia↩︎

The Ninth Computer Olympiad took place from July 3 to 8, 2004, at the Psychology Building, Bar-Ilan University, Ramat Gan, Israel, in conjunction with the 12th World Computer Chess Championship and the Computer and Games 2004 conference.
