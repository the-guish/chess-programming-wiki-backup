The Red Brick, Tilburg University 1

The Sixteenth Computer Olympiad took place from November 19 to 26, 2011, Tilburg University, Tilburg 1 , The Netherlands in conjunction with the 2nd World Chess Software Championship, the 19th World Computer Chess Championship and the Advances in Computer Games 13 conference.
