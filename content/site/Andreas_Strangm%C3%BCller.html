Andreas Strangmüller,
a German correspondence chess player and World Champion of the International Email Chess Group 1 2 in the 2004 cycle 3. Since August 2013, Andreas Strangmüller maintains the FGRL computer chess engine rating list 4, further conducting experiments concerning branching factor and engine strength as function of search time, transposition table size, processor type, and number of threads.
