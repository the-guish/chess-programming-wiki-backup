Tom Vijlbrief,
a Dutch computer scientist and senior IT Architect. He holds a M.Sc. degree in 1986 from University in Amsterdam 1. As a computer chess programmer, Tom Vijlbrief is author of the chess program Ant 2, which regularly participated at Dutch Open Computer Chess Championships and International CSVN Tournaments. More recently, Tom contributed to the Stockfish development 3, in particular to the Fishtest framework 4.
