Berta,
a private chess engine 1 written in C/C++ by Felix Schmenger. Berta regularly plays online at the Internet Chess Club 2, and has participated at various online tournaments, such as CCT tournaments and ACCA World Computer Rapid Chess Championships, most recently the WCRCC 2018.
