Steve Maughan,
a British management consultant residing in Florida 1. Steve Maughan is computer chess and Othello programmer, and author of the UCI compatible chess engines Monarch 2 and Maverick, and the Othello or Reversi program Edge Reversi 3. In March 2013, Steve started a blog on the development of his chess engine Maverick 4 5.
