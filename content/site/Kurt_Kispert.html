Kurt Kispert, (November 25, 1959 – August 14, 2020)
was an Austrian collector of dedicated chess computers and since 2001, along with Alwin Gruber, organizer of the D.A.CH-Oldie-Tournaments in Kaufbeuren 1. He further hosted and maintained Kurt´s Schachcomputer Homepage 2, which was and is an inspiration for many computer chess lovers.
