IPON,
the first pure chess engine rating list with Ponder On hosted by Ingo Bauer. IPON does not provide played games 1. In September 2013 the public IPON rating list was temporary abandoned 2 and resumed in January 2014 with some changes. In early 2019 IPON finally discontinued.
