PC Schach, (PC-Schach)
an Austrian computer chess periodical published in 1993 and 1994 in German language by the First Vienna Computer Chess Club around Andreas Mader as primary editor. PC Schach was the successor of Modul, appreciating the ongoing trend of that time for chess programs on PCs.
