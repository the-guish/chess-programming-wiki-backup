Qiang Gao,
a Chinese computer scientist and lecturer at College of Information Science and Engineering, Northeastern University, Shenyang. His research interests include computational complexity theory and game complexity, the main classes of time complexity, which are P, NP, NP-hard, NP-complete and EXPTIME, and the main classes of space complexity, that is PSCACE, NPSPACE, PSPACE-hard and PSPACE-complete. In particular, he researched on the computational complexity of n x n Chinese Chess as published along with Xinhe Xu in the ICGA Journal, Vol. 38, No. 1 1.
