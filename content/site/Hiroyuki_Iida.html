Hiroyuki Iida,
a Japanese computer scientist and computer games researcher with focus on Game-refinement theory, Opponent Model Search, and Computer Shogi. Hiroyuki Iida is Professor at Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (JAIST), and head of the games laboratory 1. Before, he was affiliated with the Shizuoka University, Hamamatsu, and was postdoctoral researcher at Maastricht University. He received his Ph.D. on Heuristic Theories on Game-Tree Search from Tokyo University of Agriculture and Technology, Tokyo 2 .

Shogi

Hiroyuki Iida is a professional 6-dan Shogi player, and co-author of the Shogi program Tacos, the four times Gold medal winner at Computer Olympiads 3 . He is member of the Board of the ICGA as Secretary-Treasurer and Section Editor of the ICGA Journal.
