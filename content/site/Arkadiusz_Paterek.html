Arkadiusz Paterek,
a Polish computer and data scientist with interests in programming, machine learning, recommender system and deep learning 1, and chess player 2. He holds a masters degree from University of Warsaw, with a thesis entitled Modeling of an evaluation function in chess 3, elaborating on the single-layer perceptron design of an evaluation function. Inspired by Michael Buro's GLEM approach 4 in the domain of Othello, Arkadiusz Paterek used his own chess program Gosu, to tune its evaluation by logistic regression.
