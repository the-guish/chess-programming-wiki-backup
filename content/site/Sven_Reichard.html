Sven Reichard,
a German mathematician, and researcher and lecturer at TU Dresden, and before research associate at University of Western Australia, Perth 1. He holds a Ph.D. in mathematics from University of Delaware, Newark under advisor Mikhail H. Klin 2. His research focuses on discrete mathematics, graph theory, finite geometry and computational discrete algebra. He is co-author of the finite geometry package FinInG 3 4, written in the GAP framework 5, which also provides an interpreted programming language. Interested in computer chess programming, Sven Reichard has written the experimental Chess Engine Communication Protocol compatible chess engine Alice 6 7 (written in GAP? No C++ 8). Further, Sven Reichard is author of the Pool program Snooze, which participated at the 11th Computer Olympiad 2006 in Turin.
