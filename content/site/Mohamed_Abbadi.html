Mohamed Abbadi,
an Italian computer scientist and Ph.D. student at Tilburg University and previously at Ca' Foscari University of Venice. His research interests include artificial intelligence, machine learning and programming languages for game development. Along with Giuseppe Maggiore, Mohamed Abbadi is primary author and project coordinator 1 of the open-source programming language Casanova 2 3 for developing Real-time strategy games, introduced at the Advances in Computer Games 13 conference in Tilburg, 2011 4.
