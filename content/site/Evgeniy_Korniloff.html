Evgeniy Korniloff, (Евгения Корнилова)
a Russian computer scientist, and games and chess programmer from the Saint Petersburg area. He is author of the chess programs OBender, previously known as QChess and Chess, and its branch Woodpecker 1 2, and the games and chess programming book Программирование шахмат и других логических игр (Programming Chess and other logical games), which includes a brief history on computer chess, explanations of game playing algorithms and programming languages like Pascal, C and C++, and source code listings of computer games and chess programs, especially QChess 3, second edition published in 2012.
