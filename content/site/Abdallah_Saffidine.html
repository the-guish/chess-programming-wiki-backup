Abdallah Saffidine,
a French computer scientist and games researcher, currently at University of New South Wales. He hold a Ph.D. from Lamsade, Paris Dauphine University in 2013 1. His research interests include Linear temporal logic (LTL) model checking, Monte-Carlo tree search, Proof-number search and General Game Playing. Along with Tristan Cazenave, Abdallah Saffidine is co-author of the Hex program Yopt, which performs Monte-Carlo Tree Search.
