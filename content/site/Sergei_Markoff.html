Sergei S. Markoff, 1
a Russian computer scientist, computer chess and games programmer. He is author of the chess program SmarThink 2 3. In 2003 Sergei introduced the Botvinnik-Markoff Extension 4. Sergei working in Sberbank leading a workgroup that focused on development of bank AI/ML systems especially in the area of speech technologies.5.
