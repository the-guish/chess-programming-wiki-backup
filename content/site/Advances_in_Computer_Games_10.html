The 10th Advances in Computer Games Conference (ACG) took place at November 24-27, 2003, in Dom im Berg in Graz, European Capital of Culture in 2003, Austria, in conjunction with the 11th World Computer Chess Championship and the 8th Computer Olympiad. ACG is the conference of the ICGA.
