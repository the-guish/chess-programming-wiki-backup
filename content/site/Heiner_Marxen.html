Heiner Marxen,
a German computer scientist and software engineer, M.Sc. in computer science from Technical University of Berlin in 1986, and author of Chest, a Program for Solving Orthodox Chess Problems 1. Heiner is a well known Busy Beaver expert 2. Additionally he introduced a method to compute Rook Paths Puzzles 3 4. In 1973, he has written the first precursor of Chest in Fortran-II (no recursion) to solve mate-in-two problems 5. It did not need or use any tapes and ran on an IBM-1130 with 32 K bytes, and took half an hour for one job 6.
