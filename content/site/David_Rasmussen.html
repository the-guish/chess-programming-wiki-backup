David Ravn Rasmussen,
a Danish computer scientist and as computer chess programmer, author of the Chess Engine Communication Protocol compatible chess engine Chezzz 1. In his 2004 Master's thesis at Technical University of Denmark, Lyngby, Copenhagen, David covers parallel search and bitboard techniques such as rotated bitboards 2.
