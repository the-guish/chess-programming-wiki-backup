Arno Nickel,
a German International Correspondence Chess Grandmaster, chess entrepreneur and publisher within his company and shop Edition Marco 1, and organizer of online Freestyle Tournaments. Arno Nickel was the Freestyle and Correspondence Chess consultant inside the Hydra-Team 2, and subsequently in 2008, became general manager of InfinityChess located in Abu Dhabi 3 .
