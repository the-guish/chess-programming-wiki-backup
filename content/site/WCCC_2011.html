The Nineteenth World Computer Chess Championship took place from November 19 to 23, 2011, Tilburg University, Tilburg 1 , The Netherlands in conjunction with the 2nd World Chess Software Championship, the 16th Computer Olympiad and the Advances in Computer Games 13 conference.
