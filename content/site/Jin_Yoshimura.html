Jin Yoshimura,
a Japanese evolutionary biologist and professor at Shizuoka University, Hamamatsu 1, and NERC Center for Population Biology, Imperial College at Silwood Park, Ascot, Berkshire, with research interests in mathematical biology, evolutionary ecology, economics, operations research and game theory.
