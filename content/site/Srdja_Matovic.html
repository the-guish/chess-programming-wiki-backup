Srdja Matovic,
a German born, Montenegrin computer scientist, software developer, and former member of the Internet Technologies Group at the Hamburg University of Applied Sciences 1. As computer chess programmer, Srdja is author of the Zeta family of chess engines, which are Zeta Dva, a conventional engine written in plain C, Zeta, written in OpenCL, a language suited for GPUs, and the 6502 retro program Zeta Vintage 2.
