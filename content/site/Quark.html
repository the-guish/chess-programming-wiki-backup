Quark,
a Chess Engine Communication Protocol compatible chess engine by Thomas Mayer, written in C++. Beside using alpha-beta, Quark used to improve about 100 Elo each in implementing a transposition table, null move pruning and an advanced evaluation along with various speed improvements 1. Over the board, Quark played the 11th World Computer Chess Championship in Graz 2003 2, as well as various IPCCCs in Paderborn, Dutch Open Computer Chess Championship, International CSVN Tournament 3 in Leiden and the BELCT 2001 4 in Berlin. It further played multiple CCT Tournaments.
