Bruno Bouzy,
a French mathematician, computer scientist and game researcher at Paris Descartes University, and along with Bernard Helmstetter and Tristan Cazenave one of the pioneers in Monte-Carlo Tree Search 1 and its application in Computer Go. Bruno Bouzy is author of the Go playing program Indigo 23, which was already subject on his 1995 Ph.D. thesis at Paris 6 University under advisor Jacques Pitrat 4.
