Hideki Kato,
a Japanese Go programmer and author of the Go playing program Fudo, formerly known as GGMC Go 1, and along with primary author Yoji Ojima co-author of the world class Go playing program DeepZen 2 applying MCTS using big patterns and root parallelization on a PC cluster, and its commercial version Tencho no Igo 3 .
