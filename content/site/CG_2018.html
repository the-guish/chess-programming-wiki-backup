CG 2018

Home * Conferences * Computer and Games 2018

The 10th International Computers and Games Conference was held on July 9 and 10 1, 2018, College of Public Affairs, San-Shia Campus, National Taiwan University, New Taipei City, Taiwan, in conjunction with the 21st Computer Olympiad.
