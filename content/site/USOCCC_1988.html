USOCCC 1988

Home * Tournaments * United States Open Computer Chess Championship * USOCCC 1988

< Prev

The Fourth United States Open Computer Chess Championship was held in Mobile, Alabama, April 8-10, 1988 - for the last time. There were four participants playing a double round robin, except the Fidelity entries had byes each other.
