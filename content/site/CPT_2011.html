CPT 2011

The fourth Chess Programmers Tournament, organized in March 2011 by Richard Pijl. It took place in De Bouwhoeve, Hoogstraten-Wortel, Belgium. Nine participants, six round swiss, time-control was 90 minutes per side and game.
