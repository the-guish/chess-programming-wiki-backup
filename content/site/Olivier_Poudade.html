Olivier Poudade

Home * People * Olivier Poudade

Olivier Poudade, (Baudsurfer/Red Sector Inc.)
a French hacker 1 and assembly programmer with focus on x86. He is member of the Red Sector Inc. (RSI) coding group 2 and leads RSI since 2008 as historic original co-founder 3. More recently, he is author of the none FIDE chess compliant minimal chess engines BootChess, released in January 2015 4, and ChessLin, published in Spring 2016 5, which even fits in 256 bytes.
