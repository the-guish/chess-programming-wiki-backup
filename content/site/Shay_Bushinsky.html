Shay Bushinsky,
an Israeli computer scientist and chess programmer. Together with Amir Ban, Shay Bushinsky is author of the world class chess engine and multiple world champion Junior. He is researcher with the CRI Institute University of Haifa. His research includes opponent modeling search as well as evaluation function tuning with supervised learning methods 1 .
