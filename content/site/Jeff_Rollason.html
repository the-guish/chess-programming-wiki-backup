Jeff Rollason,
a British computer scientist and games programmer, author of the chess program Treebeard 1, the earlier chess programs Merlin and Rasputin, and the Shogi program Shotest. Jeff is CEO and Founder of the AI Factory 2. Treebeard and Shotest are AI Factory products.
