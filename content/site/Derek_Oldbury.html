Derek Oldbury

Home * People * Derek Oldbury

Derek Edward Oldbury, (March 21, 1924 - July 9, 1994 1)
was a British draughts (Checkers) champion, competing in seven World Championship matches, and rival of Marion Tinsley. He was also a Checkers programmer, competing the 1st Computer Olympiad, 1989 with his Checker Hustler program, and the 2nd Computer Olympiad, 1990 with Checkermate, written along with Adrian Millett. Oldbury was born paraplegic 2.
