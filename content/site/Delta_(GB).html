Delta,
a microcomputer chess program by Dave Wilson which participated at the PCW-MCC 1979 as part of the Personal Computer World exhibition, London 1. Wilson later joint forces with Mike Johnson, and Delta and Mike were incorporated into their bit sliced system Advance 2.
