Weiss,
an UCI compliant open source chess engine by Terje Kirstihagen, written in C, first released in November 2019 under the GPL 3 1. Weiss was initially based on Vice and is inspired by Ethereal, Stockfish and Demolito. It is part of the OpenBench testing framework 2. Weiss had its tournament debut at TCEC Season 18 in May 2020.
