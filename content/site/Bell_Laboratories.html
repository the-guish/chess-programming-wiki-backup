Bell Laboratories, (Nokia Bell Labs)
also formerly known as AT&T Bell Laboratories and Bell Telephone Laboratories, at Murray Hill, New Jersey, USA, an American research and scientific development company, since 2016 owned by Finnish company Nokia 1.
