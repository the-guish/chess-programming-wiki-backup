Robert Pope

Home * People * Robert Pope

Robert Pope,
an American computer chess programmer, and author of the WinBoard compliant chess engines Beaches 1, an open source engine of a mixed bitboard with mailbox approach, and more recently, Abbess, based on Magic Bitboards 2.
