Harvard Cup

Home * Tournaments * Harvard Cup

The Harvard Cup Human vs. Computer Chess Challenge has been organized by Harvard University, Cambridge, Massachusetts and took place six times from 1989 until 1995, played in Boston 1 and New York City 2 .
