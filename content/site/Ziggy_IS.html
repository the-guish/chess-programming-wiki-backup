Ziggy,
an UCI compliant open source chess engine by Hrafn Eiríksson, written in Java. Ziggy represents the board with piece-lists supported by 15x8 vector attacks, and applies state of the art search enhancements like null move pruning, late move reductions, multi-cut, PVS, static exchange evaluation and a two-level transposition table, and served as test-bed for search experiments as topic in Eiríksson's B.Sc. thesis Investigation of Multi-Cut Pruning in Game-Tree Search 1. Eiríksson re-investigated multi-cut pruning with Ziggy, and found an enhancement to apply it only at expected Cut-nodes confirmed by the transposition table, that is a probe indicates a beta-cutoff without sufficient draft stored.
