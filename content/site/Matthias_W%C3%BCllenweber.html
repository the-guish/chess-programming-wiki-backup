Matthias Wüllenweber,
a German physicist, initial developer of ChessBase, the first chess game database program and the eponymous of the ChessBase company, which was founded in 1986 1 by Wüllenweber and Journalist Frederic Friedel. Matthias Wüllenweber is also developer of the Music composing software Ludwig 2 .
