Wee-Chong Oon, (Wee Chong Oon)
a Singaporean computer scientist and lecturer at Ngee Ann Polytechnic. He holds a Ph.D. in computer science from National University of Singapore in 2007, and was postdoctoral researcher at City University of Hong Kong until 2011 1. His research interests include data structures and algorithms, operations research, artificial intelligence, machine learning and interactive and abstract games like Kalah and Backgammon.
