Stuart Cracraft,
an American computer scientist and cloud DevOps, also affiliated with Apple Inc. and Wells Fargo 1. As computer chess programmer, Stuart Cracraft is primary author of GNU Chess Version 1, starting the development in the mid 80s 2, and author of Gazebo 3 which was later incorporated into GNU Chess 5. In 1984, Stuart Cracraft published on bitboard move generation in the ICCA Journal 4.
