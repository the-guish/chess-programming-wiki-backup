Soft Jacqueline Bras,
a chess engine by Bruno Bras, apparently derived from Bras' earlier engines Pacque Expert, Chess Christy and Kristy Lenix 1. Soft Jacqueline Bras played the Massy 2004 and Massy 2005 French Programmers Tournaments, and the FCCC 2004 French Computer Chess Championship. __FORCETOC__
