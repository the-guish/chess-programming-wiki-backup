Munjong Kolss,
a Korean born, German biologist, chess and correspondence chess player 1 and computer chess programmer. He graduated in biology at University of Kiel in 2003, received a Ph.D from University of Fribourg, Switzerland, in 2007, was postdoc at Bielefeld University 2, and is now deputy head of the department for evaluations, University of Cologne 3. Along with his twin brother Muntsin, Munjong is author of the chess engine Ikarus.
