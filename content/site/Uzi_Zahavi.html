Uzi Zahavi,
an Israeli computer and data scientist, Ph.D. from Bar-Ilan University in 2009 on the artificial intelligence topic of [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iterative_deepening_A* single agent search] 1 under Ariel Felner 2. He further worked on field service optimization problems, combining features of the vehicle routing problem (VRP), scheduling problems and the general assignment problem (GAP).
