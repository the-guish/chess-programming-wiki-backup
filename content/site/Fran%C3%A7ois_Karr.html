François Karr,
a French professional software developer, lead programmer and technical director at Dontnod Entertainment. He was R&D manager at Ubisoft, specialized in racing game developments, co-founded Babylon Software 1 in 1999, and was affiliated with Darkworks. He created a physical module based on a very realistic collision engine such as Ipion 2 3. Along with chess advisor Jean-Philippe Karr, François is author of the chess programs BugChess and BugChess2.
