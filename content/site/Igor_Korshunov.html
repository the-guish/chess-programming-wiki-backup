Igor Korshunov

Home * People * Igor Korshunov

Igor V. Korshunov,
a Belorussian chess programmer, and author of the chess program WildCat 1, which won the 1st Computer Chess Championship of CIS Countries in 2008, and the Russian draughts program Kallisto 2 3. Along with Jury Osipov, Igor Korshunov is co-author of the chess program Belka 4. In May 2010, Igor Korshunov introduced his new open source engine Murka 5, in July 2013 available as version 3.0 6. Igor is moderator of the Kasparov computer chess forum 7.
