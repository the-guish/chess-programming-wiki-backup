Günter Reinemann, (January 26, 1942 - July 16, 2018 1)
was a German chess official, computer scientist, during the 60s affiliated with the department of computing technology at TU Merseburg and head of their data center 2, as well as computer chess expert and co-author of computer chess books. Günter Reinemann was honorary president and senior chess consultant of the Chess Federation of Saxony-Anhalt 3, and co-founder of the Emanuel Lasker Society in 2001 4 5.
