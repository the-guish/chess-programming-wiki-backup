Genesis,
a Chess Engine Communication Protocol compatible chess program by Eli David, written in C++, first released in April 2002, initially available from Leo Dijksman's WBEC Ridderkerk site. Genesis is based on a NegaScout/PVS framework using history heuristic and transposition table. Like its successor Falcon, Genesis was designed especially for academic research 1, notably on verified null-move pruning 2.
