Stefan Edlich,
a German computer scientist and engineer, and senior lecturer at the University of Applied Science, Beuth Hochschule für Technik Berlin. His interests include NoSQL Web 2.0 and software engineering, cloud computing, data-science, and programming languages, such as Java, Ruby and D, and chess programming 1. He is author of some experimental chess playing entities, the private or no longer available EdlChess, written in Ruby 2 3 as well as Rasbojnik, written in D 4 5, and most recently, the UCI compliant open source chess engine and random mover Finny, written in Python 3 using the Python-chess library 6.
