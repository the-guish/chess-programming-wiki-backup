Frédéric Louguet,
a French computer chess programmer, journalist and writer, in 2006 co-founder and now CTO of Ranch Computing, a Render farm located in Paris with huge GPU resources 1 2. Frédéric Louguet is author of the chess programs Chess Wizard, three time winner of the French Computer Chess Championships, and the experimental program Raptor 3. Frédéric Louguet was editor of the French computer chess magazine La Puce Echiquéenne, which appeared in 17 issues from 1992 until 1997 4, author of LCT II, the Louguet Chess Test II 5 and author of books about various computer related issues.
