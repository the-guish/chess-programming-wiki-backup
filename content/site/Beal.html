Beal

Home * Engines * Beal

Beal,
Don Beal's first chess program, which played the First World Computer Chess Championship in Stockholm, running on a CDC 6600 mainframe computer at Queen Mary Collage. It shared a telephone line from Stockholm to London with Master 1.
