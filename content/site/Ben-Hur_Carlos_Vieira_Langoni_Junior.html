Ben-Hur Carlos Vieira Langoni Júnior,
a Brazilian computer scientist and software engineer holding a Bachelor's degree in computer science from Universidade Salgado de Oliveira, São Gonçalo, Rio de Janeiro 1. As computer chess programmer, Ben-Hur Carlos Vieira Langoni Júnio is author of the UCI compliant open source chess engine RedQueen 2.
