DOCCC 1999

Home * Tournaments * Dutch Open Computer Chess Championship * DOCCC 1999

The 19th Dutch Open Computer Chess Championship took place at Denksportcentrum Leiden. It was an eleven round swiss tournament at two consecutive week ends, October 30-31 and November 6-7, 1999.
