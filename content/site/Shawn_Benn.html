Home * People * Shawn Benn

Shawn Benn,
an American computer scientist, software and database engineer, from 1989 until 1996 affiliated with the Department of Computer Science, University of Maine, and since then, holding various positions in the industry 1. In 1992, along with Danny Kopec, he recalibrated the Bratko-Kopec Test using six commercial chess programs, Chessmaster 3000, Excel 68000, MChess, Sargon IV, Sargon V and Zarkov 2.5 2.
