Ira D. Baxter,
an American entrepreneur, Ph.D. in computer science from University of California, Irvine, 1990, and CEO 1 2 of Semantic Designs 3. He is the principal architect of Semantic Designs' Design Maintenance System (DMS) 4, principal designer and compiler implementer of PARLANSE 5, a parallel programming language 6, and architect and principal engineer on CloneDR 7, an AST-based Clone Detection technology.

Software Dynamics

Involved with computing since 1966, Ira Baxter started Software Dynamics in 1976, a systems software house, where he designed compilers, time-sharing and distributed network operating systems, and the chess program SD Chess written in compiled Basic to run on a 6800 based microcomputer 8, A program called Chess D by Ira Baxter was on the Stand-By list of the WCCC 1983 in New York 9.
