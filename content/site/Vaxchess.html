Vaxchess,
a chess program by Tony Guilfoyle and Richard Hooker, running on a MicroVAX II minicomputer. It played two tournaments in 1986, the WCCC 1986 in Cologne, and the ACM 1986 in Dallas. Vaxchess was written in C and assembly 1, and searched about 1K nodes per second 2.
