Confucius,
a chess program by Martin Anders and Hinrich Büker 1, developed in 1996 when the authors were graduate students at Hamburg University of Applied Sciences. Confucius played the IPCCC 1997, running on a Sun Ultrasparc 2.

Etymology

In 1994, both authors spent half a year in Shanghai as exchange students, which gave the inspiration to name the engine after the famous Chinese philosopher 3.
