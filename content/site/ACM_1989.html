ACM's Twentieth North American Computer Chess Championship (NACCC), Reno, Nevada, USA, November 12-15, 1989. The tournament was held in conjunction with the ACM/IEEE Supercomputing Conference in the Reno Convention Center. For the final winner, see the controversial discussion between Hans Berliner and Feng-hsiung Hsu at rec.games.chess 1.
