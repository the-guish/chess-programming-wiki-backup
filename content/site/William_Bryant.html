William Bryant

Home * People * William Bryant

William Bryant,
an American computer chess programmer 1 2 3. He developed a chess interface for the Macintosh using the PowerPlant toolkit, first released in 1998 along with a TSCP port dubbed MacTSCP 4, soon superseded by his own engine for the Macintosh chess program Screamer 5.
