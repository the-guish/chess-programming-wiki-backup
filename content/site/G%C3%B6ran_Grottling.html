Göran Grottling,
a Swedish journalist at times affiliated with Göteborgs-Posten 1, until 1997 member of the SSDF and dedicated chess computer aficionado and tester, in 1984 initiator of the SSDF rating list 2, and author of computer chess articles and interviews published in ICCA Journal, PLY, Modul and Computerschach und Spiele.
