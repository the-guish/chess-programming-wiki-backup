ACCA World Computer Rapid Chess Championship

Home * Tournaments * ACCA World Computer Rapid Chess Championship

The ACCA World Computer Rapid Chess Championship are organized by the Americas' Computer Chess Association ACCA since 2007. The format is Rapid Chess (G/25+4), played on-line over the internet (ICC).
