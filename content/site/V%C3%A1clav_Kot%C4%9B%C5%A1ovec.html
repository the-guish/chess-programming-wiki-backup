Václav Kotěšovec, (Vaclav Kotesovec)
a Czech mathematician, fairy chess composer and programmer. In 2005, he was awarded with the title of a FIDE International Master for Chess Composition, and in 2010 with the title of International Judge for Chess Compositions 1. Václav Kotěšovec is author of the solving and composing software VKsach 2, and has published in ICGA Journal on King and Two Generalised Knights against King 3. He has also contributed to the On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences 4.
