Quadruple pawns

As mentioned by Marcel van Kervinck 3 , former Draughts World Champion Jannes van der Wal arranged to get the seldom case of a Quadruple pawn versus HiTech, the machine by former Correspondence chess World Champion Hans Berliner performing B*:
