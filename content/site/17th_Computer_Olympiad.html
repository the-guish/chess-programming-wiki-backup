17th Computer Olympiad 2013, (Brain & Mind Sports Computer Olympiad)
held from August 12 to 18, 2013, the Collaboration Complex, Kyōsei-kan, Keio University, Yokohama, Japan in conjunction with the WCCC 2013, the WCSC 2013, and the 8th International Computers and Games Conference.
