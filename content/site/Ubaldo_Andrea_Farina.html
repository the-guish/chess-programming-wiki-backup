Ubaldo Andrea Farina,
an Italian computer chess programmer. Author of the chess engines Fauce 1 and Chiron, competing at the WCCC 2006 in Turin, and winner of the 2010 Italian Computer Chess Championship within the 1st Italian Open Chess Software Cup and subsequent tournaments, commercially available since October 2011 2 3 .
