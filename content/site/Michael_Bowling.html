Michael H. Bowling,
an American computer scientist and full professor at the University of Alberta with research focusing on artificial intelligence, machine learning, games, and robotics. He received his Ph.D. on multiagent learning under advisor Manuela Veloso at Carnegie Mellon University, where he was involved in the RoboCup initiative. At UofA, he is leader of the Computer Poker Research Group 1, recently applying deep learning to the game of Poker with the AI agent DeepStack 2 3 4.
