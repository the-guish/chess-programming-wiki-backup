10th Computer Olympiad

Institute of Information Science 1

  1. Institute of Information Science, Academia Sinica, Taipei, Image from CO 10 and ACG 11, Pictures 09/12/2005↩︎

The Tenth Computer Olympiad took place from September 3 to 6, 2005 at Institute of Information Science, Academia Sinica, Taipei, Taiwan, in conjunction with the Advances in Computer Games 11 conference 1. The organization of both events by the ICGA was in the hands of Academia Sinica, in particular Tsan-sheng Hsu and his staff, supported by the IKAT (Institute for Knowledge and Agent Technology) team from Maastricht University. Jaap van den Herik served as tournament director, assisted by Tsan-sheng Hsu. The Chinese Chess tournament with a strong field of 14 participants was led by Tony Marsland 2.

The 10th Computer Olympiad was the first one not directly associated with a (Western) computer chess event, typically the World Computer Chess Championship - the WCCC 2005 already took place from August 13 to 21 in Reykjavík, Iceland.
