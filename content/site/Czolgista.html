Czolgista,
a private chess engine by Tomasz Kazimierski 1. As mentioned by Grzegorz Sidorowicz 2, Czolgista exclusively uses Multi-Cut rather than Null Move Pruning. Czolgista played the CCT5 and CCT7 CCT Online Tournaments in 2003 and 2005 respectively.
