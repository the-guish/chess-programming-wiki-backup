DOCCC 2008

Home * Tournaments * Dutch Open Computer Chess Championship * DOCCC 2008

< Prev Next >

The 28th Dutch Open Computer Chess Championship took place November 14 to 16, 2008, at Denksportcentrum Leiden. With 10 participants a 9 rounds robin tournament was played, 90 minutes per player for the whole game.
