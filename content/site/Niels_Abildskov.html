Niels Abildskov,
a Danish computer scientits with interests in computer chess programming. He is author of the UCI compliant open source chess engines Copper 1 and Loki. Copper, his first chess engine trial was heavily influenced by Vice, is actually buggy and superseded by Loki 2. __FORCETOC__
