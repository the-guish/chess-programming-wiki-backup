Dorpsgek,
a Chess Engine Communication Protocol compliant chess engine by Dan Ravensloft, written in C, previously available as open source licensed under the GNU General Public License 1. Dorpsgek versions were named after Cocktails in alphabetic order, starting with Ambrosia released in July 2016 2.
