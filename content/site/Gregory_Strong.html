Home * People * Gregory Strong

Gregory Strong,
an American professional software developer, Chess enthusiast, and author of ChessV 1, an open source engine that plays over 50 chess variants with various board sizes, as well as Orthodox Chess 2. He is further working on a chess engine dubbed Quadrox 3 4.
