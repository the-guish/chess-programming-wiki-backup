Pallav Nawani,
an Indian electrical engineer with a M. Tech degree in satellite technology from Indian Institute of Science, Bangalore, game developer and CEO of IronCode Gaming Pvt. Ltd. 1. He is author of the Chess Engine Communication Protocol compliant open source chess engine Natwarlal 2. __FORCETOC__
