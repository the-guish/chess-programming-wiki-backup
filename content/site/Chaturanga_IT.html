Chaturanga,
a Chess Engine Communication Protocol aka WinBoard compatible chess engine by Stefano Malloggi. Chaturanga played the WCCC 2006, and various Italian Computer Chess Championship 1. The name Chaturanga is derived from the ancient Indian game which is presumed to be the common ancestor of the games of Chess, Shogi and Chinese Chess 2.
