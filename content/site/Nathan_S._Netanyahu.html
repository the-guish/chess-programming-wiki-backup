Nathan S. Netanyahu,
an Israeli computer scientist and professor at the Department of Computer Science 1 Bar-Ilan University 2, Ramat Gan, Israel. Nathan S. Netanyahu was local organizer of the 12th World Computer-Chess Championship 2004, the 9th Computer Olympiad and 4th International Conference on Computers and Games at the Bar-Ilan University in Rama Gan, assisted by his Ph.D Student Eli David 34.
