Stefan Knappe,
a German economist, club chess player 1 and avocational computer chess programmer. He is author of the chess program Matador, which also participated as Lokalmatador 2 at the BELCT 2001 in Berlin. Along with Dieter Bürßner et al., Stefan was member of the ChessCoach - Team, developing chess training software for Palm and Pocket-PC 3, also affiliated with the Inside Chess project focusing on web development 4. __FORCETOC__
