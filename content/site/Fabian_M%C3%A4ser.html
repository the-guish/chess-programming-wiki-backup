Fabian Mäser,
a Swiss computer scientist, FIDE Master in chess 1, and former member of Jürg Nievergelt's research group at Institute of Theoretical Computer Science at ETH Zurich. He defended his Ph.D. thesis Divide and Conquer in Game Tree Search on combinatorial game theory in 2001 2. He is currently affiliated with the Ergon software company in Zürich 3.
