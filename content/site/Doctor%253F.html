Doctor?,
is the commercial successor of Bobby II by Hans-Joachim Kraas and Günther Schrüfer. It was market by ChessBase as 16-bit engine in 1995 as analysis engine for ChessBase/Windows 1.0, and in 1998 as 32-bit add-on engine of the Fritz 5.32 package 1 2. Doctor? is written in C and was therefor available for various hardware platforms and operating systems. According to Ingo Althöfer, who used Doctor? in his triple-brain experiments, it was the first engine which implemented a k-best mode. Doctor?'s strengths were mainly due to strategy and unsual pawn structures in endgames. The engine uses fractional plies with a depth increment in iterative deepening of 1/3 ply 3.

Program Characteristics

4

  • STC-Search (solution tree cost oriented search) 5
  • Ample chess knowledge in evaluation function and search heuristics.
  • Positional playing style with tactical surprises.

  • Implemented on Atari ST, PC (Unix, Win3.x, Win95/98, WinNT), Mac, Sun, BiiN
