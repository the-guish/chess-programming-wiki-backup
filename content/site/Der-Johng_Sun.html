Der-Johng Sun

Home * People * Der-Johng Sun

Der-Johng Sun,
a Taiwanese computer scientist at Department of Computer Science and Information Engineering, National Chiao Tung University, Hsinchu. His research interests include computer games, distributed computing and web technology 1.
