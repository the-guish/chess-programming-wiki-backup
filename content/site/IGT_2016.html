The I.G.T. 2016 (International Gsei Tournament) was planned to be held in Norcia Ospitalia Il Capisterium, Norcia, Perugia, Italy on September 10-11, 2016. Due to the earthquake which struck Norcia on August 24, 2016 1 2, the IGT was held at Accademia Scacchistica Romana 3 , Rome 4 .
