The ACM's Eighth North American Computer Chess Championship (NACCC) was held on October 15 to 17, 1977 at the Olympic Hotel in Seattle, Washington, United States.

In the evening after Round 3, Tournament Director David Levy held a simultaneous exhibition against all twelve programs, winning 10, drawing one, and losing to Chess 4.6. The next day, after the tournament was over, he played two games of speed chess against Chess 4.6 and Duchess and lost both 1 .
