David Maxwell (Max) Chickering,
an American computer scientist and Microsoft employee working in Microsoft Live Labs and formerly as member of the Machine Learning and Applied Statistics (MLAS) group of Microsoft Research. He received his Ph.D. in computer science from the University of California, Los Angeles. His research interests covers search algorithms and practical applications of machine-learning algorithms.
