Erik van het Hof,
a Dutch computer scientist and software engineer with a M.Sc. degree from University of Twente in 1998 1, and affiliations in Fort Lauderdale, The Hague and Hong Kong 2. Along with Hermen Reitsma, Erik van het Hof is co-author of the chess engines BugChess aka QueenMaxima 3 and Maxima2 4.
