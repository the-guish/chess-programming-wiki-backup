Gianluca Cisana,
an Italian photographer and freelance programmer 1. He is author of the WinBoard compliant chess engine BremboCE. The chess engine's name is related to the river Brembo in the Province of Bergamo, situated near Gianluca's home 2. Gianluca further hosts a chess tactics training site 3, providing the Ideachess Android app 4.
