Tim Foden,
a British software engineer and computer chess and games programmer. He is author of the WinBoard compliant chess engine Green Light Chess 1, and was also involved in robot auto racing simulation (RARS) 2. More recently, he worked on the Go playing program Fluke, performing Monte-Carlo tree search 3.
