The 12th Aegon Man-Machine tournament took place from April 16 to 18, and 21 to 23, 1997, at Aegon Headquarters in The Hague, Netherlands.Winner with a 100% score was Yona Kosashvili, followed by Yasser Seirawan and Johan van Mil with 5½/6 each.Best programs were Kallisto and Rebel 1 with 4½.
