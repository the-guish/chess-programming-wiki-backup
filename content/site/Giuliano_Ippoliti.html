Giuliano Ippoliti,
an Italian computer scientist, chess player 1, and CISO for a cloud provider in France 2 3. He holds a B.Sc. in mathematics and computer science, and a M.Sc. in telecommunications engineering from University of Pisa in 2003. In 1998, he was finalist at the Italian Mathematics Olympiads, and the national qualifier of the International Physics Olympiads 4.

Computer Chess

Giuliano Ippoliti is author of the open source chess engine GiuChess, written in C 5, and the Perl module Chess-Play to play chess games, calculate legal moves, and to use a search algorithm 6.
