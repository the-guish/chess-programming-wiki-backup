Paul Verhelst,
a Dutch computer chess expert, who published and maintained interesting chess 1 and chess programming sites 2. Ed Schröder once mentioned the 0x88 square difference lookup as Paul Verhelst way 3, which was used and described by Paul Wiereyn 4 and, according to David Kittinger, was originally attributed to Mikhail Botvinnik 5. __FORCETOC__
