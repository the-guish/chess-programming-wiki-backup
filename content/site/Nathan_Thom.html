Nathan Thom,
an Australian software engineer at Suncorp with degrees in Information technology and microelectronic engineering from Griffith University 1. As a computer chess programmer, Nathan is author of the UCI compliant engine LittleThought 2, and the Windows tournament manager program for UCI based chess engines, LittleBlitzer 3.
