Shawn Chidester,
an American senior software engineer at Attensity 1, and as computer chess programmer author of the chess engines CACIO (early 90s in Turbo Pascal 2), Clubfoot, its bitboard counterpart Bitfoot, the experimental Clunk 3, Bumblebee 4, and the Senjo C++ UCI adapter. Senjo simplifies the UCI protocol implementation of engines written in C++, as demonstrated by Clubfoot, Bitfoot and Bumblebee 5.
