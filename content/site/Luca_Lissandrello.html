Luca Lissandrello,
an Italian chess player 1, graduated surveyor, self-employed systems and programming consultant, and as avocational chess programmer author of the chess engine Neurone, written in Visual Basic .NET 2. He is active member of the G 6 or GSei, the Gruppo Scacchi e Informatica or the Italian Computer Chess Association, editor of their web sites, as well as tournament director.
