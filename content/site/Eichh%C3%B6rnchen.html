Eichhörnchen, (Eichhoernchen, белка, belka)
a private chess engine 1 written by Wieland Belka. Eichhörnchen played the First Italian Open Chess Software Cup, 2010 in Carugate and the WCRCC 2019. It further played Olivier Deville's OpenWar and ChessWar tournaments 2.
