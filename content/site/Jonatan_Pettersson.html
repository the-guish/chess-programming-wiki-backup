Jonatan Pettersson,
a Swedish computer scientist and Java developer. He is author of the chess engine Mediocre, written in Java 1, and maintains the Mediocre Chess blog, covering the development of his engine and various topics on chess programming, also recently announcing he is working on a new bitboard based Java engine 2.
