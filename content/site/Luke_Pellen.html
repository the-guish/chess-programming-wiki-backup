Luke Pellen,
an Australian programmer, game developer, artist, photographer, graphic designer, musician, composer, actor, comedian, writer, and 2001 Loebner Prize finalist. In 1999 he started to develop the chess playing neural networks program Octavius, and was in 2002 invited by Kluwer Academic Publishers, now Springer Science+Business Media, to write a chapter for the Artificial Intelligence Textbook Parsing the Turing Test: Philosophical and Methodological Issues in the Quest for the Thinking Computer 1.
