Jean-Yves Audibert,
a French statistician, computer scientist, and lecturer at École Normale Supérieure de Cachan and École nationale des ponts et chaussées 1. His research topics include machine learning, specially Probably approximately correct (PAC) learning, Multi-armed bandits, Non-parametric statistics, Sequential prediction 2 with limited feedback and Computer vision. Jean-Yves Audibert is contributor to the Go playing program Mogo, using Monte-Carlo Tree Search which uses patterns in the simulations and improvements in UCT 3.
