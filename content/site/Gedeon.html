Gedeon

Home * Engines * Gedeon

Gedeon X,
a Chess Challenger clone 1 by Bela Gedeon, which was allowed to play the WMCCC 1983 in Budapest as local entry. According to Frederic Friedel 2, the modification was only due to the power supply. The machine had a 6502B processor @ 3MHz, 32 KiB ROM, 16 KiB RAM, apparently a Sargon based Chess Challenger by Dan and Kathe Spracklen. __FORCETOC__
