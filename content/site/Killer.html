Killer,
a chess program written in Turbo Pascal by Mario Carbonell Martinez, German Blanquer and Alvaro Benlloch. Killer regularly played the Spanish Computer Chess Championship in the 90s, and became tied runner-up along with Che at the SCCC 1993.
