Home * People * Bojun Guo

Bojun Guo, (alias noobpwnftw)
a Caribbean pirate1, computer chess and computer Xiangqi developer, supporter and sponsor, as well as active member in computer chess forums and social media. He donates high-quality hardware for some communities such as Fishtest 2, OpenBench 3, TCEC 4, and Talkchess.

7-man Syzygy Bases

In an 5-month attempt in 2018 - supported by Ronald de Man - Bojun Guo has generated all 7-men tables of Syzygy Bases 5 6.

Cloud Databases

Bojun Guo is author of the Chess Cloud Database Query Interface 7 and the Xiangqi Cloud Database Query Interface 8. The APIs will provide online position analysis as well as EGTB results where available 9.
