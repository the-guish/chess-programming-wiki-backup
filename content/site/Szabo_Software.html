Szabo Software

Home * Organizations * Szabo Software

Szabo Software,
was an American software company located in Borrego Springs, California. Szabo Software was active in the mid and late 80s, conducted by Barbara and Alexander Szabo, primary business was developing and distribution of chess software, notably their own program TechMate for the Atari ST 1.
