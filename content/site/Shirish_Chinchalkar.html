Shirish Chinchalkar,
an Indian American mathematician, mechanical engineer, computer scientist, and senior analyst at Moody's Investors Service 1. He holds a Ph.D. in mechanical engineering from Cornell University and an M.A. in mathematical finance from New York University, and gained experience in numerical and high-performance computing, and computational finance, where he applied Monte Carlo methods, numerical optimization, and parallel computing while teaching and researching at Indian Institute of Technology Bombay and Cornell University 2.

Number of Chess Positions

In 1996, Shirish Chinchalkar determined 10^46.25 as upper bound for the number of reachable chess positions 3.
