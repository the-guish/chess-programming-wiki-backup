The 12th Advances in Computer Games was held from May 11-13, 2009, at Palacio del Condestable, Pamplona, Navarre, Spain, in conjunction with the 17th World Computer Chess Championship and the 14th Computer Olympiad. ACG is the conference of the ICGA.
