Blitz Monster

Home * Engines * Blitz Monster

Blitz Monster,
an experimental version of Novag's Super Constellation or its successor Constellation Expert 1 2 by David Kittinger, competing with three entries at the WMCCC 1985 in Amsterdam. During the Hong Kong human Blitz tournament March 1984, Super Constellation got 19/28 only playing Black, with 5 points versus International Masters in the 2355 to 2474 range, and was spontaneously called Blitz Monster 3.
