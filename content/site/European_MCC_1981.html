European MCC 1981

Home * Tournaments * European Microcomputer Chess Championship * 2nd European MCC 1981

< Prev Next >

The inofficial 2nd European Microcomputer Chess Championship took place September 1981 in London, United Kingdom during the Personal Computer World Show, also known as the 4th PCW Microcomputer Chess Championship.
