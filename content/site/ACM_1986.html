The ACM's Seventeenth North American Computer Chess Championship (NACCC), took place from November 2-6, 1986, Anatole Hotel, Dallas, Texas, USA. Simultaneously, in the Dallas Infomart, the 6th WMCCC took place with a compatible time schedule for all those participating both events.
