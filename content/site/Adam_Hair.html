Adam Hair,
an American computer chess aficionado, notably working with Miguel A. Ballicora on testing Gaviota, and member of CCRL, the Computer Chess Rating Lists. In January 2012, he published a survey of evidence from both perspectives concerning the Rybka Controversy and ICGA Investigations, at times as hosted by Ed Schröder 1. Adam Hair served as moderator in CCC.
