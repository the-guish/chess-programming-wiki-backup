KChess,
a family of commercial chess programs by Rob McDonell, written in Delphi, market by his software development business company Ark Angles 1. In the early 90s KChess' development was inspired by Kaare Danielsen's Turbo Chess program, and the KChess engine is still largely Turbo Chess with some code improvements 2. KChess has its own GUI to run under Windows, KChess Advance 3 and specially the more sophisticated KChess Elite 4 focus on educational features and are suited for use with screen reader software for the blind or visually impaired 5.
