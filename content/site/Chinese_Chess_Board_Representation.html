The Chinese Chess (Xiangqi) board is a grid of 10 horizontal lines (ranks) and 9 vertical lines (files) with total of 90 points or cells. The board can use almost all board representations of chess such as Piece Centric, Square Centric (except 0x88) and Hybrid Solutions.
