Home * Engines * Ga Tech CP

Ga Tech CP,
the Georgia Tech chess program by Lawrence Futrell, written in Algol. Ga Tech CP played the ACM 1973 in Atlanta, Georgia and ran on a Burroughs B-5500 of the university 1.
