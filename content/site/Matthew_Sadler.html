Matthew D. Sadler,
an English chess grandmaster, chess writer and two-time British Chess Champion, in 1995 and 1997 1. In 2017, he analyzed the computer games of AlphaZero versus Stockfish 2, and along with Natasha Regan co-authored the book Game Changer which details the lessons that can be learnt from how AlphaZero plays chess 3. Since TCEC Season 14 in 2019, Matthew Sadler reports on the Superfinals of the Top Chess Engines Competition, published on TCEC and the ICGA Journal.
