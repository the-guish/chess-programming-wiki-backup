Marvin Harry Solomon,
an American mathematician, computer scientist and professor at University of Wisconsin-Madison, Madison, Wisconsin. He holds a Ph.D. with the thesis title Theoretical Issues in the Implementation of Programming Languages 1, 1977 from Cornell University 2. His research interests covers object-oriented database systems, software development support environments, distributed operating systems, computer networks, design and implementation of programming languages, and programming language theory 3. In 1989 and 1990, along with his Ph.D. student Igor Steinberg, he published on Steinberg's thesis topic of Design and Analysis of Parallel Algorithms for Game-Tree Search 4.
